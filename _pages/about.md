I am a Doctoral Researcher at [Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IP Paris)](https://www.ip-paris.fr/), working on **generative vision, controllable video, and image synthesis**. I am particularly interested in building **physically plausible and realistic video and image models**.

**Research interests:**

- Video diffusion and flow-matching models
- Reinforcement learning and preference alignment for generative models
- Controllable and identity-preserving image and video synthesis
- Physically plausible and realistic human video generation
{: .interests}

Previously, I did my M.Sc. in Computer Science at Saarland University and wrote my master's thesis at the [Max Planck Institute for Informatics](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/), advised by [Prof. Christian Theobalt](https://people.mpi-inf.mpg.de/~theobalt/) and [Dr. Rishabh Dabral](https://rishabhdabral.github.io/). Before that, I was a research intern at Huawei's Munich Research Center, working on safe and identity-preserving diffusion models. I received my B.Sc. in Computer and Systems Engineering from Alexandria University.
