Salaheldin Y. Mohamed

I am a Doctoral Researcher at Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IP Paris), working on generative vision, controllable video, and image synthesis. I am particularly interested in building physically plausible and realistic video and image models.

Research interests:

  • Video diffusion and flow-matching models
  • Reinforcement learning and preference alignment for generative models
  • Controllable and identity-preserving image and video synthesis
  • Physically plausible and realistic human video generation

Previously, I did my M.Sc. in Computer Science at Saarland University and wrote my master’s thesis at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, advised by Prof. Christian Theobalt and Dr. Rishabh Dabral. Before that, I was a research intern at Huawei’s Munich Research Center, working on safe and identity-preserving diffusion models. I received my B.Sc. in Computer and Systems Engineering from Alexandria University.

I am looking for research internships and visiting positions for 2027. Feel free to reach out.
